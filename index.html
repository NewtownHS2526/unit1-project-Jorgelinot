Ilia Topuria

"El Matador"

Welcome to my fan page for Ilia Topuria, one of the most exciting UFC fighters in the world. Known for his precision, confidence, and knockout power, Topuria has become a rising star and a true inspiration for many fans.

Biography

Ilia Topuria was born on January 21, 1997, in Halle, Germany, to Georgian parents. He represents both Georgia and Spain in the UFC. Topuria started training in mixed martial arts at a young age and quickly made a name for himself due to his discipline, skill, and determination.

He made his UFC debut in 2020 and rapidly rose through the ranks with an undefeated record. In 2024, he became the UFC Featherweight Champion, making history as the first Spanish fighter to win a UFC title.
